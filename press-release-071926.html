JULY 19, 2027

Officers Respond to Reports Near Dry Creek Road

RED MESA, AZ — Officers responded to the 600 block of Dry Creek Road at approximately 11:43 PM Saturday following several reports of unusual activity near the roadway.

Officers searched the area and contacted two residents. No suspect was located.

Residents are reminded not to approach persons behaving suspiciously and to contact the department immediately.

At approximately 2:17 AM, officers were dispatched to the same location following a second report.

The responding officer reported no persons present at the scene.

Report associated with incident RM-27-0719-44. Additional information withheld pending review.
