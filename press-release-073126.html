JULY 31, 2027

Missing Juvenile Located

RED MESA, AZ — A juvenile reported missing on July 29 has been located and safely returned to family members.

The department thanks members of the public for their assistance and reminds residents to contact law enforcement immediately when a child is believed to be missing.

No further information is available at this time.
