AUGUST 9, 2026

Arrest Made Following Residential Burglary

RED MESA, AZ — Detectives with the Criminal Investigations Division arrested a 32-year-old Red Mesa resident following an investigation into several residential burglaries.

Property recovered during the investigation has been returned to its owners. Additional charges remain pending.
