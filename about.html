About the Department

The Red Mesa Police Department is committed to protecting the lives, property, and constitutional rights of all persons within the Red Mesa community.

Our Goal For The Community

The Red Mesa Police Association has been dedicated to serving the Greater Arizona area for the better part of eighty years. Our founder Larry Lubertson had one dream from the day he decided his life would be dedicated to the law. That dream was to unite and build trust with the humans of his surroundings; Resulting in vastly improved communication with each and every citizen inhabiting the west coast as of today and tomorrow. Public safety is our most immediate concern and we have no such priority to allow crime to get as bad as it was before Larry took charge because as he spoke, "We focus on bringing justice first. Its just the way it should be."
