AUGUST 14, 2027

Traffic Collision on State Route 87

RED MESA, AZ — Officers responded to a two-vehicle collision on State Route 87 at approximately 8:16 AM Friday.

Two individuals were transported to Red Mesa Regional Medical Center with non-life-threatening injuries. The roadway was reopened at approximately 10:04 AM.

The collision remains under investigation.
